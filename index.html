    How it works

    Two modules, one platform

    User Module

    Every user builds and edits their own portfolio (About, Skills, Projects), browses vacancies posted by the admin, applies to the ones they want, and sees notifications when the admin flags a good match for them.

    Admin Module

    The admin posts and manages job vacancies, browses every user's portfolio, reviews and updates the status of applications, and can directly notify a specific user about a vacancy that matches their skills.
